Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Music Mind Reader ("the game", "the app") is a free party game played in a browser at musicmindreader.com. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Playing without an account

You never need an account to play. When you create or join a game, the app generates a random player ID and stores it, together with the name you type in, in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your own device. This is only used to recognise your device if you reconnect to the same game (e.g. after a dropped connection), and is never sent anywhere except to the game session you're playing in.

Optional accounts

You can optionally create a free account (via Google sign-in, or an email address and password) so your name is remembered between games and you can save songs you hear to your own private lists. This is handled by Firebase Authentication (provided by Google). Creating an account stores your display name and a short discriminator number (e.g. "Alex #0421") — this is visible to other players so they can tell apart people with the same name; your email address itself is never shown to anyone else. If you use Google sign-in, Google's own privacy policy also applies to that sign-in step — see Google's Privacy Policy.

Saved song lists

If you're signed in, you can save a song someone else submitted into your own private list for a category of your choosing. These lists are private — visible only to you, never to other players, both for ordinary privacy and because a visible list could hint at what you might submit in a future round. Saving a song never records who originally submitted it.

What data is stored, and where

While a game is in progress, the following is stored in a shared real-time database (Firebase Realtime Database, provided by Google) so that everyone in the same game can see live updates:

  • The player name you type in
  • The song titles/links submitted during a round
  • Guesses and ratings submitted during a round
  • Running scores for the game

This data is scoped to the specific game room (identified by its room code) and is visible to other players in that same room, which is the entire point of the game — it is not used for any purpose outside of running that game session, and it is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

If you create an account, your profile (display name and discriminator) and your saved song lists are also stored in the same database, scoped to your account rather than to one game, and are not shared with third parties either.

YouTube playback

To play submitted songs, the app searches for a matching video using the YouTube Data API and plays it using the official YouTube embedded player (the YouTube IFrame Player API), shown visibly on screen with YouTube's own branding. Google/YouTube may collect data about that playback according to their own privacy policy — see Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Advertising

The app currently has no ads. If ads (e.g. Google Ads) are added in the future, they will never be placed on or over the YouTube video player itself, in line with YouTube's developer policies — this page will be updated if that changes.

Cookies

The app does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Only the browser's local storage is used, as described above, to remember your player identity within a game.
